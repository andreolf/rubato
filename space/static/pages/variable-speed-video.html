What it is

Normal speed controls give you one number for the whole video. Variable speed, also called dynamic speed or smart speed, gives every passage its own number. The video is re-timed once and saved as a new file, so it plays the same way in any player, on any device, with no extension or special app.

How Rubato decides the speed of each passage

Three layers, each overriding the one before:

  1. Sound and picture. Pauses and flat, halting stretches are found from the audio and from how much the picture changes. These only ever make a passage faster. A pause while something is happening on screen is not treated as dead air.
  2. The transcript, by phrase. The recording is transcribed and sentences that announce themselves are recognised: "the key takeaway", "three things you need to know", "number two", "in summary", figures and percentages. Those slow down. "Can you hear me" and "thanks for watching" speed up.
  3. The transcript, read by an AI agent. When Rubato is used as a skill or connector, the agent reads the transcript and marks the real conclusions, the complete lists and the tangents, including the ones nobody labelled.

The default speeds

What is happeningSpeed
A takeaway, a list, key numbers1.0x
Everything ordinary1.2x
Logistics, hesitation, flat stretches1.5x
Dead air2.0x

All four are adjustable. If you normally watch at 1.5x, set the ordinary speed to 1.5x and raise the others to match.

What it is good for, and what it is not

It suits recordings where someone talks: lectures, conference talks, meetings, interviews, podcasts, tutorials and narrated screen recordings.

It does not suit music, films, or anything where timing is the point. The built-in phrases are English; other languages are transcribed, but fewer passages are recognised unless an agent reads the transcript.
