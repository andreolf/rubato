    How dynamic speed works

    Interesting means what is being said, not how loud it is. Rubato reads the recording before it touches the speed.

    1 · Listen

    Find the dead air

    Pauses and flat, halting stretches are measured from the audio and the picture. These only ever get faster.

    2 · Read

    Find what matters

    The recording is transcribed. Takeaways, lists and key numbers are kept at normal speed. Logistics and hesitation are sped up.

    3 · Render

    One continuous video

    Every passage gets its own speed. Voices keep their pitch, nothing is cut, and subtitles can be retimed to match.

    Each passage gets the speed it deserves

    These are the defaults. Every one of them is adjustable.

    What is happeningSpeed
    A takeaway, a list, key numbers1.0x
    Everything ordinary1.2x
    Logistics, hesitation, flat stretches1.5x
    Dead air2.0x

    Questions

    How do I speed up only the boring parts of a video?

    Upload it above. Rubato transcribes the recording, keeps takeaways, lists and key numbers at normal speed, plays ordinary passages at 1.2x, filler at 1.5x and silence at 2x. You get one continuous video back, with nothing cut.

    How is this different from watching at 1.5x?

    A flat 1.5x rushes the parts you need to follow and still wastes time on the parts you do not. Dynamic speed gives each passage its own speed based on what is being said.

    Is this the same as silence removal or jump cuts?

    No. Silence removers cut content out, which causes visible jumps. Rubato never removes anything; it only changes speed, so the video stays continuous and subtitles can be retimed exactly.

    Will voices sound high-pitched?

    No. The audio is time-stretched, so voices keep their natural pitch at every speed.

    What about music, or videos with background music?

    Changing the speed of music every few seconds makes the tempo lurch. When the sound never goes quiet and no key passages are found, Rubato uses one flat speed and tells you. Speech over background music still works, since the transcript drives the picks.

    What happens to a video sent through the Claude connector?

    The same as an upload: it is processed on the server, the source file is deleted when processing ends, and the result is deleted after 30 minutes. The connector needs a direct link to the media file, not a page that plays it. See the privacy policy.

    How big is the download if I run it myself?

    The command-line tool, skill and local MCP server download a speech-to-text model on first use: about 150 MB for the default model, cached and reused afterwards. Nothing is downloaded to use this page or the hosted connector.

    What kind of video does it work on?

    Anything where someone is talking: lectures, talks, meetings, interviews, podcasts, tutorials and narrated screen recordings. It is not meant for music videos, and raw footage with repeated takes needs editing first.

    Which formats and sizes can I upload?

    MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3, M4A, WAV and most other formats ffmpeg reads, up to {{MAX_MINUTES}} minutes and {{MAX_MB}} MB in this demo. The result is an MP4 (H.264 with AAC audio), or M4A for audio. The skill and command-line tool have no limit.

    Does it work in languages other than English?

    Transcription works in most languages. The phrases this demo looks for are English, so for other languages use the skill, where the agent reads the transcript itself, or add your own phrases on the command line.

    What happens to my file?

    It is processed on the server, the upload is deleted as soon as processing ends, and the result is deleted after 30 minutes. Do not upload confidential recordings to a public demo; run Rubato locally for those.
