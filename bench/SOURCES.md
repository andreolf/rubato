## Suggested Real Video Sources

When selecting test videos, ensure they are:
1. Openly licensed (CC-BY, CC0, Public Domain)
2. Talking-head format with clear speech
3. Educational or presentation content
4. 1-5 minutes in length

### Where to Find Suitable Videos

#### Internet Archive (archive.org)
- Search: "lecture public domain"
- Search: "presentation creative commons"
- Filter by: Creative Commons licenses

**Example Collections**:
- Academic lectures with CC licensing
- Conference talks released under CC-BY
- Educational content from universities

#### Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org)
- Search for: "lecture" or "presentation" 
- Filter by: Videos with CC-BY or CC0 licenses

#### YouTube (via yt-dlp)
```bash
# Check video license
yt-dlp --get-title --get-license <VIDEO_URL>

# Download CC-BY videos only
yt-dlp --get-license <VIDEO_URL> | grep -i "Creative Commons"
```

**Note**: Always verify the actual license on the video page, not just tags.

## License Compliance

All videos used in this benchmark must comply with open licensing terms:

✓ **Acceptable Licenses**:
- Public Domain / CC0
- CC-BY (with proper attribution)
- CC-BY-SA (with proper attribution)

✗ **Not Acceptable**:
- All Rights Reserved (copyrighted)
- CC-BY-NC (non-commercial restriction)
- CC-BY-ND (no derivatives restriction)
- Unlicensed content

When in doubt, **do not include the video** in the benchmark.

## Attribution

When using CC-BY licensed videos, provide attribution:

```

available at [URL]
```
