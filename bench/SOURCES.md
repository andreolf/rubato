## About LibriVox

LibriVox (librivox.org) is a free public domain audiobook project. All recordings are in the public domain in the USA and many other countries. The works themselves (books) are also in the public domain.

**Mission**: "To make all books in the public domain available, for free, in audio format on the internet."

## About MIT OpenCourseWare

MIT OpenCourseWare (ocw.mit.edu) publishes virtually all MIT course content under Creative Commons licenses. Most content uses CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0, allowing use with attribution for non-commercial purposes.

**Attribution Requirements**: 
- Include the author's name
- Include the course/work title  
- Note the license (CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0)
- Link to the license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

The 2-minute excerpts used from longer works may also qualify as fair use under:
- Purpose: Educational testing and benchmarking

- Amount: Small portion of full work (2 min from 60+ min)
- Effect: No market impact (promotes original work)

## Replacement Guidelines

When selecting additional test videos:

### Acceptable Licenses
- Public Domain / CC0
- CC-BY (with proper attribution)
- CC-BY-SA (with proper attribution)
- CC-BY-NC-SA (non-commercial use with attribution)

### Not Acceptable
- All Rights Reserved (copyrighted without open license)
- CC-BY-ND (no derivatives allowed)
- Unlicensed content
- Content with unclear licensing
